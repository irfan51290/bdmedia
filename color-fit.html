The Problem

Most people don't know which colors actually suit them — not just personal taste, but which shades genuinely flatter their skin tone, hair, and eyes. Color season analysis exists (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) but it's buried behind expensive consultants or vague online quizzes that give you no actionable next step. Even if you know your season, your existing wardrobe is a mystery: does this blazer actually work for you?

How It Works

  • Upload a selfie — Claude AI analyzes your skin undertones, hair, and eye color
  • Get mapped to 1 of 12 color seasons with your personalized palette
  • Add wardrobe items — each piece is scored against your season
  • AI generates full outfits from your existing clothes using only season-friendly colors
  • Every outfit comes with a Pantone-coded color story
  • Inspiration feed curated to your season and chosen vibes

What I Learned

Multi-modal AI prompting is a craft — getting Claude to return consistent, structured color analysis from a photo took many prompt iterations. I also had to genuinely understand color theory (Itten's seasonal system, warm vs cool undertones) before I could prompt for it accurately. Building full-stack with Next.js 15, Railway, and SQLite in a week meant making fast architectural decisions and living with them.

Built With Claude

Claude API (claude-3-5-sonnet) powers all image analysis — a single photo returns a structured JSON with undertone classification, season match, and confidence score. Claude Code scaffolded the entire Next.js + SQLite stack in hours. The outfit generation prompt chains the user's season palette against their wardrobe inventory to pick harmonious combinations and generate the Pantone color story.
