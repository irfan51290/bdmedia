The Problem

Career quizzes are a dime a dozen — most spit out a generic result and forget you the moment you close the tab. I needed a way to identify people who are genuinely stuck on their career direction and want real guidance, not just a personality label.

How It Works

  • User answers 10 questions about goals, ambitions, and work style
  • Responses are scored and mapped to a curated future career path
  • Every submission is captured in Airtable with full response data
  • Automations score each lead based on profile fit
  • Highest-fit profiles get a personal outreach — not a mass email

What I Learned

Building lead gen into a quiz flow without it feeling like lead gen. The scoring logic took the most time — getting it to produce results that feel insightful rather than random. Connecting Airtable automations to trigger personal follow-ups was the part that made it actually useful as a business tool.

Built With Claude

Used Claude Code to scaffold the Next.js project, design the scoring algorithm, and wire up the Airtable integration. Vercel deployment was live in minutes. Week 3 was the first time I shipped a full front-end app with no prior Next.js experience — and it doubled as a real business tool.
