The Problem

Freelancers run their business across a patchwork of disconnected tools: invoices in spreadsheets, leads tracked in WhatsApp threads, projects scattered across sticky notes. Nothing connects. Revenue leaks because invoices never get sent. Clients go cold because follow-ups fall through the cracks. There's no single place to see whether the business is actually growing — or quietly dying.

How It Works

  • Choose your experience level on sign-up — guided mode for new freelancers, full access for experienced ones
  • Sales: lead pipeline, AI-generated proposals from a brief, scope of work templates, referral tracker
  • Finance: one-click invoices that auto-fill from your profile, credit & debit ledger, expense tracking, savings prompts on every payment received
  • Projects: Kanban board with clock in/out per task, revision round counters, and an honest effective hourly rate for every project — fixed or hourly
  • Marketing: content calendar, AI post ideas tailored to your niche, portfolio tracker, bio builder
  • Every morning at 9am, a Telegram message surfaces what needs attention: overdue invoices, cold leads, approaching deadlines

What I Learned

Designing for two personas — complete beginners and experienced freelancers — from a single codebase forced real product decisions, not just UI toggles. The finance module was the hardest to model: separating "owed" from "received", handling partial payments, and nudging savings without being preachy required several iterations. Building Supabase RLS across 13 interconnected tables also surfaced just how complex per-user data isolation becomes at scale — what looks clean in a schema diagram gets messy fast when you add row-level policies to every join.

Built With Claude

Claude Code designed the full 13-table Supabase schema with RLS policies, the sidebar navigation architecture, and the authentication flow. The most complex decision was the dual-persona onboarding — Claude proposed a single experience_level flag in the user profile that drives which dashboard components render, letting the same codebase serve both beginner and pro modes without branching the logic. The AI proposal pipeline — turning a two-line brief into a full scope document — was the most iterative prompt engineering of any project so far.
