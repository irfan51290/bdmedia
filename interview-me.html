The Problem

Job interviews are high-stakes but almost impossible to prepare for realistically on your own. Reading guides helps, but you don't know how you'll actually perform until you're in the room — when it's too late. Coaching is expensive, practising with friends is awkward, and recording yourself lacks any meaningful feedback. Most people walk in underprepared.

How It Works

  • Upload your CV and set your target role — used to personalise every session
  • Paste a job description; AI extracts role, company, and key requirements
  • AI researches the company and generates context-aware interview questions
  • A pre-interview briefing primes you with themes, tips, and your strengths to highlight
  • Choose Mock Mode (end-to-end, no feedback) or Practice Mode (scores after each answer)
  • An AI interviewer speaks questions aloud via TTS; you answer via microphone
  • AI scores Content and Delivery for each answer, with model answers provided
  • Results page shows overall score, a full written report, and a downloadable cheat sheet

What I Learned

The browser's Web Speech API is far less reliable than I expected — transcription quality varies wildly across browsers and microphone inputs, which forced a lot of defensive handling. The trickier challenge was AI evaluation: prompting a rubric where a 7.4 feels earned and actionable — not arbitrary — took significantly more iteration than the rest of the app combined. Multi-step wizard state across server components and client components in Next.js 16 also surfaced edge cases I hadn't seen in earlier projects.

Built With Claude

Claude Code designed the full architecture — the four-step session wizard, Supabase schema, AI evaluation pipeline, and TTS integration. The most nuanced work was the evaluation prompt: Claude had to score answers on two independent axes (content quality and delivery quality), generate specific feedback, and produce a model answer — all from a single API call. Getting that to produce consistently calibrated, useful output was the real engineering challenge of this project.
