What's inside

Four departments. One dashboard.

Everything a freelancer needs to operate like a real business — without the overwhelm of enterprise tools built for teams.

Sales

Lead pipeline, AI-generated proposals, scope of work templates, and a referral tracker so word-of-mouth never gets lost.

Finance

One-click invoices that auto-fill from your profile, a credit & debit ledger, expense tracking, and savings prompts on every payment.

Projects

Kanban board, clock in/out per task, revision round counters, and an honest effective hourly rate for every project — fixed or hourly.

Marketing

Content calendar, AI post ideas tailored to your niche, portfolio tracker, and a bio builder for your socials.

Telegram briefings

Every morning at 9am, get a message on what needs your attention — overdue invoices, cold leads, upcoming deadlines.

Guided onboarding

New to freelancing? A step-by-step checklist teaches you what running a freelance business actually involves.

Contracts made simple

Pre-built scope of work templates by service type — protect yourself from scope creep before work even begins.

Know your real rate

For fixed-price projects, FLT shows your actual hourly rate based on time logged — no judgement, just the number.

The loop

From lead to paid, automatically.

One signed scope of work creates the project. One completed project creates the invoice. Nothing falls through the cracks.

1

Lead comes in

Added to your pipeline, proposal generated by AI from a short brief.

2

Scope signed

Project auto-created with deliverables, deadlines, and revision limits.

3

Work happens

Clock in/out, log expenses, track revisions — all in one place.

4

Get paid

One-click invoice pulls in time, expenses, and your profile details.
