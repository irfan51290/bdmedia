The Problem

Job searching is exhausting. Scrolling through endless listings, tailoring your resume for each role, and losing track of what you applied for — it's a full-time job in itself. I wanted a faster, smarter way to move through the funnel.

How It Works

  • Jobs are presented as swipeable cards — swipe right to apply, left to skip
  • On swipe right, GPT-4 rewrites your resume to match the job description
  • All applications tracked automatically in Supabase with status updates
  • Built with React Native and Expo for iOS-first experience
  • Supabase handles auth, storage, and real-time application tracking

What I Learned

React Native gesture handling is deceptively complex. Getting smooth, Tinder-like card physics right required digging into reanimated and gesture handler in ways I hadn't anticipated. Mobile development has its own set of sharp edges.

Built With Claude

Claude Code designed the full app architecture — swipe mechanics, AI resume tailoring pipeline, and Supabase schema. This was the most technically complex week: three surfaces (mobile UI, AI integration, backend) had to work together from day one.
